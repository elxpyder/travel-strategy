    COMPLETE DOCUMENTATION PACKAGE¶

    PHASE 1: RESEARCH & MARKET ANALYSIS (5 files)¶

    Product Ideas¶

    • What it contains: 10 flagship service offerings for UHNW Bilbao market
    • Key sections: Service descriptions, target clients, unique value propositions
    • Why you need it: Understand service diversity; customize for specific clients
    • Use case: When designing custom proposal for client, reference relevant journey types

    Competitor Analysis¶

    • What it contains: 11 direct competitors analyzed; threat assessment; positioning gaps
    • Key sections: Competitive matrix, strengths/weaknesses, opportunities for differentiation
    • Why you need it: Understand competitive landscape; justify why you win
    • Use case: During investor pitch or board presentation; competitive positioning arguments

    Partnership Opportunities¶

    • What it contains: 50+ potential partners mapped across 14 categories
    • Key sections: Hotels, destinations, transport, expert brokers; by region/type
    • Why you need it: Know who to partner with; supplier ecosystem map
    • Use case: Partnership development phase (Month 1-2); vendor sourcing

    Global Agency Benchmarks¶

    • What it contains: 12 world-class luxury travel agencies analyzed; service models compared
    • Key sections: Virtuoso member analysis, service innovations, pricing strategies
    • Why you need it: Learn best practices; avoid mistakes; benchmark your service design
    • Use case: Operational planning; service delivery design; quality standards

    Research Sources¶

    • What it contains: All sources cited in research (articles, reports, websites)
    • Key sections: Source listing by topic (market size, competitors, partners, benchmarks)
    • Why you need it: Verify research; update with fresh data quarterly
    • Use case: Fact-checking; building credibility with stakeholders

    • What it contains: 5-tier client acquisition framework; 90-day action plan; KPI tracking
    • Key sections:
    • Positioning shift (from sales → partnership)
    • Basque business clubs & networking venues
    • Consultative sales approach
    • Retention & expansion strategies
    • 90-day weekly breakdown
    • Success metrics
    • Why you need it: Complete go-to-market playbook

    • What it contains: 15 verified UHNW prospects with detailed profiles

    • How to approach (connection pathways, pitch angle)

    • Use case: Client prospecting; warm intro identification; proposal customization

    • What it contains: Complete service delivery blueprint; partnership strategy; staffing model

    • Concierge + partner broker business model

    • Partnership infrastructure (Tier 1-4 partners, negotiation points)
    • Staffing model (roles, responsibilities, growth triggers)
    • Technology stack
    • Quality control & risk management
    • Why you need it: Understand how to execute; operations playbook
    • Use case: Operational planning; staffing decisions; partnership negotiations

    • Strategy 4: Value-based
    • Detailed revenue examples for each
    • Comparison matrix
    • Implementation roadmap
    • Why you need it: Understand pricing framework; avoid pricing mistakes
    • Use case: Board decision; pricing validation with clients; justification for chosen model

    Budget & Financial Model¶

    • What it contains: Complete financial projections; investment required; profitability timeline
    • Key sections:
    • Startup costs (€35K breakdown)

    • Cash flow timeline

    • Use case: Budget approval; cash runway planning; profitability justification

    HOW TO USE THIS PACKAGE¶

    SCENARIO 1: You're Preparing to Pitch Internally (Board/Leadership)¶

    Read in order: 1. 11_executive_summary_master_report.md (full) 2. 10_budget_financial_model.md (full) 3. 12_dashboard_key_metrics.md (financial tables) 4. 13_presentation_strategy.md (AUDIENCE 1: Internal Team section)

    Then create: - PowerPoint deck using Audience 1 template from 13_presentation_strategy.md - Pull financial data from 10_budget_financial_model.md - Pull market/competitive data from 11_executive_summary_master_report.md

    SCENARIO 2: You're Meeting Your First Potential Client¶

    Read in order: 1. 07_initial_client_targets.md (find your prospect's profile) 2. 13_presentation_strategy.md (AUDIENCE 2: First Potential Client section) 3. Reference specific custom offer from client profile

    Then: - Print one-pager (design using 13_presentation_strategy.md FORMAT B) - Bring case study (reference 04_global_agency_benchmarks.md for examples) - Follow conversation flow from 13_presentation_strategy.md

    SCENARIO 3: You're Negotiating Partnerships¶

    Read in order: 1. 08_delivery_operational_model.md (partnership categories, commission structure) 2. 03_partnership_opportunities.md (specific partner research) 3. 13_presentation_strategy.md (AUDIENCE 3: Partnership section)

    Then: - Create partnership proposal using template from 13_presentation_strategy.md - Reference specific commission rates from 08_delivery_operational_model.md - Send follow-up with terms from same document

    SCENARIO 4: You're Planning Operations¶

    Read in order: 1. 08_delivery_operational_model.md (full) 2. 10_budget_financial_model.md (staffing and operating costs section) 3. 12_dashboard_key_metrics.md (operational checklist)

    Then: - Create detailed operations manual from 08_delivery_operational_model.md - Establish systems (CRM, trip software, project tracking) - Hire operations coordinator based on staffing framework - Set KPIs based on 12_dashboard_key_metrics.md

    SCENARIO 5: You're Tracking Progress Monthly¶

    Use: - 12_dashboard_key_metrics.md (monthly KPI template) - Update actual metrics against targets - Track acquisition pipeline, revenue, profitability - Monitor risk dashboard - Adjust strategy based on real results

    SCENARIO 6: You're Pitching to Investors¶

    Read in order: 1. 11_executive_summary_master_report.md (full) 2. 10_budget_financial_model.md (full, especially financial scenarios) 3. 02_competitor_analysis.md (competitive advantage) 4. 07_initial_client_targets.md (market validation with real prospects) 5. 13_presentation_strategy.md (AUDIENCE 4: Investor section)

    Then: - Build pitch deck using Audience 4 template - Include 3-year financial model from 10_budget_financial_model.md - Highlight unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback period) - Show competitive moat from 02_competitor_analysis.md - Reference real prospects from 07_initial_client_targets.md

    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS¶

    Q: Which document should I share with potential investors?¶

    A: Start with 11_executive_summary_master_report.md (full document). If they ask for details, provide: - 10_budget_financial_model.md (detailed financials) - 07_initial_client_targets.md (market validation) - 02_competitor_analysis.md (competitive advantage)

    Q: I want to modify the pricing. Where do I look?¶

    A: 09_pricing_strategies.md (all models with pros/cons)
    Then update: 10_budget_financial_model.md (revenue projections change)

    Q: Can I share all these documents with my team?¶

    A: Yes, but consider audience: - Sales team → 06_client_acquisition_strategy.md + 07_initial_client_targets.md - Ops team → 08_delivery_operational_model.md + 12_dashboard_key_metrics.md - Finance team → 10_budget_financial_model.md + 12_dashboard_key_metrics.md - Leadership → All of it

    Q: How detailed should I get when presenting to a client?¶

    A: Share positioning + custom offer only (from 07_initial_client_targets.md). Don't share: - Competitive analysis - Financial model - Internal operations - Pricing strategy

    Keep it about them, not about you.

    Q: What if our market changes or competitors shift?¶

    A: Update in this order: 1. 02_competitor_analysis.md 2. 10_budget_financial_model.md (adjust projections) 3. 11_executive_summary_master_report.md (update summary) 4. 12_dashboard_key_metrics.md (update targets)
