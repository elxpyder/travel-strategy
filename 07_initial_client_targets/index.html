    1. FRANCISCO J. RIBERAS MERA¶

    Profile: Executive Chairman, Gestamp Automoción

    BACKGROUND & BIOGRAPHY¶

    • Wealth: €1.6B+ (Spain's 18th richest billionaire, listed 2024)
    • Age: ~70s
    • Education: Law & Economics degree (multi-disciplinary thinker)
    • Family Background: Multi-generational Basque industrial dynasty
    • Father: Francisco Riberas Pampliega (founded Gonvarri steel trading in 1958)
    • Brother: Juan María Riberas Mera (jointly owns Gestamp with him, 50/50 split)
    • Sons: At least 2 adult sons, both involved in family business or professional careers
    • Family Trait: Basque industrial mentality (hard work, family loyalty, community respect)
    • Company: Gestamp Automoción
    • Founded: 1991 (spinoff from family's Gonvarri steel company)
    • Scale: 22,000+ employees, 96 factories worldwide
    • Revenue: €11B+ annually
    • Major clients: Volkswagen, Renault, major automakers
    • Public company (IPO 2017 on Madrid stock exchange)
    • Holds majority through family investment arm (Acek Desarrollo y Gestión Industrial)

    PERSONAL LIFESTYLE & TASTES¶

    • Work Ethic: Legendary—works constantly, presence at board/operations is paramount
    • Family Values: Highly family-oriented (Basque cultural trait), wants quality time with extended family
    • Leisure Style: Minimal—prefers efficiency over relaxation; when traveling, mixes business with pleasure
    • Known Interests:
    • Japanese manufacturing culture & kaizen philosophy (respects Japanese industrial approach)
    • Automotive innovation & EV technology
    • Fine dining (Michelin restaurants, but less about leisure, more about efficiency)
    • Golf (occasional, more for business entertaining than hobby)
    • Philanthropy (Basque tradition—likely supports local causes)
    • Basque cultural preservation
    • Travel History: Limited leisure travel; mostly business trips to Germany, Japan, USA, Mexico
    • Social Circles: Inner circle is family + trusted business associates; not a "socialite" type
    • Decision Style: Transactional, values-driven, old-school Basque honor code (handshake = binding)
    • Pain Points:
    • Time poverty (no time for leisure, but recognizes need for family bonding)
    • Guilt about missing children's/grandchildren's upbringing due to work demands
    • Wants to leave "legacy trips" that bond generations without disrupting business
    • Seeking meaningful experiences, not frivolous vacations

    HOW TO APPROACH¶

    • Connection: Through brother Juan María or through Gestamp board member relationships
    • Pitch Angle: Frame as "family legacy investment" not vacation
    • Key Message: "2-week journey that brings your whole family together—designed to be efficient with minimal business disruption"
    • Tone: Respectful, direct, business-like (avoid fluff)
    • Best Time: Off-season for auto manufacturing (summer July-August)

    CUSTOM OFFER: "Legacy Journey"¶

    "10-14 Day Multi-Generational Industrial Heritage + Adventure Experience" - Participants: 8-12 family members (sons, daughters, grandchildren, spouse) - Destinations: Japan (Hokkaido) + Singapore - Itinerary: - Japan Days 1-3: Tokyo automotive facilities tour (Nissan, Toyota manufacturing), meet with Japanese executives (business mentorship angle) - Japan Days 4-7: Hokkaido wilderness experience (skiing, hot springs, family bonding, zero business talk) - Days 8-10: Singapore luxury stopover (shopping, fine dining, wellness for spouses) - Special Touch: Hire professional filmmaker to document entire trip as family archive/heirloom - Why It Works: Respects his time constraints, mixes intellectual stimulation (business culture) with family bonding, creates documented legacy
