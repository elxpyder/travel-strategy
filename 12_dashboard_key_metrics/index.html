    🎯 TARGET MARKET PROFILE¶

    Market Composition¶

    Segment Size Avg Travel Spend Growth Rate
    UHNW (€30M+) 2,500 households €15,000+/day 12% CAGR
    HNW (€10-30M) 8,500 households €5,000-8,000/day 14% CAGR
    VHNI (€1-10M) 25,000 households €2,000-3,000/day 8% CAGR
    Market Total 36,000+ households €2,000–€15,000/day 11% CAGR

    Your target: UHNW segment only (2,500 households, most profitable)

    Why Bilbao UHNW Market?¶

    • ✅ Highest per-capita income in Spain (€38K+)
    • ✅ Strong family businesses (Gestamp, BBVA, ITP Aero, etc.)
    • ✅ Concentrated wealth in geographic region
    • ✅ Underserved by luxury agencies (no local specialist)
    • ✅ Strong cultural ties (family-oriented, relationship-driven)

    Recommendation: HYBRID MODEL¶

    Component Value
    Membership Tier Premium (€7,500/year)
    + Commissions 12% average (partner rates)
    + Markup 12% on trip costs
    Total margin per client 35–40%
    Recurring revenue base €7,500/year × 8 clients = €60K
    Trip revenue layer €120K+ annually
    Total potential €180K–€200K per year

    Why this model: - ✅ Predictable recurring revenue (membership) - ✅ Justifiable pricing (membership = exclusivity) - ✅ UHNW psychology (like private clubs, premium cards) - ✅ Retention driver (annual renewal) - ✅ Scaling efficiency (members incentivized to book more)

    📈 REVENUE MODELS: TRAJECTORY¶

    Scenario A: Conservative (Slow Growth)¶

    Year 1: 5 clients → €98K revenue
Year 2: 8 clients → €250K revenue  
Year 3: 12 clients → €400K revenue

⚠️ Risk: Acquisition bottleneck, referral dependency
✓ Advantage: Fewer execution risks, high service quality

    Scenario B: Moderate (Steady Growth) ← RECOMMENDED¶

    Year 1: 8 clients → €138K revenue
Year 2: 14 clients → €446K revenue
Year 3: 20+ clients → €700K+ revenue

✓ Balanced growth, warm intros + events
✓ Sustainable scaling, manageable execution
✓ Realistic client acquisition pace

    Scenario C: Optimistic (Aggressive Growth)¶

    Year 1: 10 clients → €175K revenue
Year 2: 18+ clients → €600K revenue
Year 3: 30+ clients → €1.1M revenue

⚠️ Risk: Over-extension, quality degradation
✓ Advantage: Market leadership, scale advantage
✓ Requires: Hiring, process automation, partnerships

    Planning assumption: Moderate scenario (Scenario B)

    📞 DECISION FRAMEWORK¶

    Quick Decision Guide¶

    Q: Should we launch this service? - ✅ YES if: You have €35K-40K runway, want €50K+ Year 1 profit, can commit 15-20 hrs/week - ❌ NO if: Limited capital, already overcommitted, uncertain about UHNW market

    Q: Should we use Premium tier (€7,500/year)? - ✅ YES — Balances price/value, targets majority of market, high margins (35-40%) - ⚠️ Consider Entry (€2,500) if clients resist; Elite (€15K) only for multi-gen families

    Q: Should we hire staff immediately? - ❌ NO — Start solo, hire operations coordinator at 4-5 clients - ✅ YES (hire at Month 4-6) if tracking ahead of plan

    Q: Should we do cold outreach? - ❌ NO — UHNW clients respond to warm intros only - ✅ DO warm introductions exclusively; build reputation first

    Q: How many clients to be "successful"? - Minimum: 3 clients (break-even) - Target: 8 clients (€52K profit, business is real) - Stretch: 12 clients (€120K+ profit, hire 2nd manager)
