    Delivery & Operational Model: Premium UHNW Service¶

    Positioning Statement¶

    "Basque insider expertise + global network: We design bespoke journeys for ultra-wealthy Bilbao residents, combining deep local knowledge of our region's culture, values, and networks with exclusive access to world-class experiences globally."

    Service Delivery Model¶

    1. THE CONCIERGE + PARTNER BROKER HYBRID¶

    You don't operate experiences yourself—you curate and broker them through vetted partners while providing white-glove concierge service.

    Your Role:¶

    • Relationship manager (client-facing, understand their needs deeply)
    • Curator (design itinerary, select partners, manage logistics)
    • Problem solver (on-call during travel, handle issues)
    • Network connector (facilitate introductions, arrange VIP access)

    Partner Structure:¶

    • Tier 1 Partners: Luxury hotel groups (Four Seasons, Rosewood, Belmond, Six Senses)
    • Tier 2 Partners: Destination specialists (local guides, experience operators)
    • Tier 3 Partners: Activity providers (adventure companies, wellness centers, art advisors)
    • Tier 4 Partners: Transportation (luxury airlines, private jets, yacht charters)

    Commission Model (Typical):¶

    • Hotels: 10-15% commission
    • Activities/experiences: 20-25% commission
    • Transportation: 5-10% commission
    • Your markup: Additional 10-15% on total package price

    Operational Workflow¶

    Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1-2)¶

    1. Initial consultation (1.5-2 hours)
    2. Understand client's travel style, interests, constraints
    3. Budget range, dates, participants, goals

    4. Pain points (what do they NOT want?)

    5. Debrief & strategy (internal)

    6. Create brief profile/preference document
    7. Identify 2-3 destination options aligned to their interests
    8. Preliminary budget framework

    Phase 2: Proposal Design (Week 2-3)¶

    1. Create custom itinerary
    2. 7-14 day detailed day-by-day plan
    3. Include research on destinations, activities, local culture

    4. Arrange expert introductions (scientists, craftspeople, executives, artists)

    5. Assemble vendor quotes

    6. Hotel bookings with complimentary upgrades negotiated
    7. Activity reservations
    8. Transportation quotes
    9. Meals at key restaurants

    10. Insurance & contingency (2% buffer)

    11. Present proposal

    12. Written proposal with visuals (20-30 pages)
    13. In-person or video presentation
    14. Adjust based on feedback

    Phase 3: Booking & Logistics (Week 3-8)¶

    1. Secure all reservations
    2. Hotel confirmations & special requests
    3. Activity bookings
    4. Flights/transportation
    5. Travel insurance

    6. Visa support if needed

    7. Create trip book

    8. Printed/digital trip guide
    9. Restaurant reservations with special notes
    10. Contact info for all vendors
    11. Emergency contacts

    12. Local tips & cultural notes

    13. Client pre-travel briefing

    14. Video call walkthrough
    15. Q&A session
    16. Final logistics confirmation

    Phase 4: On-Travel Support¶

    1. Before departure
    2. Send daily briefing email with weather, tips, what to pack
    3. Reminder of reservations

    4. VIP confirmations with vendors

    5. During travel

    6. WhatsApp/email available 24/7
    7. Proactive check-ins (morning text with day's plan)
    8. Problem resolution

    9. Real-time recommendations if plans change

    10. Post-travel

    11. Follow-up call (within 1 week)
    12. Request feedback (refine for future trips)
    13. Professional photography/video package
    14. Next travel planning discussion

    Phase 5: Retention & Expansion¶

    1. Annual check-in
    2. Seasonal travel ideas
    3. New experiences aligned to interests

    4. Referral bonuses if they introduce friends

    5. Loyalty perks

    6. Priority reservations at partner hotels
    7. Exclusive events (art viewings, wine tastings, cultural events)
    8. Special pricing for repeat bookings

    Key Partnership Categories to Establish¶

    A. LUXURY HOTELS (Core Infrastructure)¶

    Negotiate net rates + commission: - Four Seasons (30+ properties globally) - Rosewood Hotels (19 properties, ultra-luxury) - Belmond (38 properties, heritage focus) - Six Senses (15+ properties, eco-luxury focus) - Peninsula Hotels (13 properties, Asian focus) - Fairmont (80 properties, destination appeal) - Mandarin Oriental (33 properties, urban luxury)

    Negotiation points: - 10-15% net rates (vs. public rates) - Complimentary room upgrades - $100-200 USD daily spa/dining credits - Late checkout - Your white-label presentation

    B. DESTINATION SPECIALISTS (Local Execution)¶

    By destination (examples): - Japan: Luxury DMC with Hokkaido specialists (manufacturing tours, hot springs, family experiences) - Patagonia/South America: Conservation-focused operators (rainforest research, wildlife, sustainable tourism) - Iceland/Norway: Geothermal & renewable energy specialists - Florence/Venice: Art advisor networks + private collection access - Kenya/Tanzania: Conservation-focused safari operators - Maldives: Private island specialists, diving, marine biology experts

    Negotiate: - Commission on activities (20-25%) - Exclusive experiences (not available to public) - VIP treatment (guides, private vehicles, permits)

    C. TRANSPORTATION PARTNERS¶

    • Luxury airlines: Business/first class codes + agent commissions (Lufthansa, Air France, Swiss)
    • Private jet: NetJets, VistaJet, Blade (for ultra-time-pressed clients)
    • Yacht charters: Camper & Nicholsons, Alquiler de Yates (Mediterranean focus)
    • Luxury car rental: Hertz Presidents Circle, local premium services

    D. EXPERT/EXPERIENCE BROKERS¶

    • Art advisors: International art auction house networks
    • Michelin restaurants: Direct relationships for reservations
    • Adventure companies: Heliskiing, mountaineering, expedition operators
    • Wellness centers: Spa brands (Como Shambhala, HEAL)
    • Educational: University contacts, research center introductions

    Staffing Model for Premium Tier¶

    Minimum Viable Team:¶

    1. Premium Services Manager (1 FTE) — Your role or hire
    2. Client relationships
    3. Itinerary design
    4. Vendor coordination

    5. Travel support during trips

    6. Operations/Logistics Coordinator (0.5 FTE, can be part-time)

    7. Booking confirmations
    8. Document preparation
    9. Backup support during travel

    10. Billing/commission tracking

    11. Existing infrastructure:

    12. Use existing agency infrastructure (billing, insurance, compliance)
    13. Leverage existing staff for standard travel components
    14. Outsource design research to freelance researchers ($50-100/hour)

    Optional Scaling (Year 2+):¶

    • Second premium manager (if you reach 8-12 active clients)
    • Dedicated designer/researcher
    • Local concierge in key destinations (Madrid, Barcelona, London)

    Technology & Systems¶

    Required (Minimum):¶

    1. CRM System (if not already using)
    2. Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive
    3. Track client preferences, history, communications

    4. ~€50-200/month

    5. Trip Planning Software

    6. ToursByLocals, Ferryman, or custom spreadsheet system
    7. Organize itineraries, vendor contacts, logistics

    8. ~€0-100/month

    9. Communication:

    10. WhatsApp Business API for client support
    11. Email (Gmail Business or similar)

    12. Free or included

    13. Document Management:

    14. Google Drive or Dropbox for trip books, proposals
    15. Included in existing infrastructure likely

    Optional (Enhancement):¶

    • Custom client portal (White-label solution: ~€1,000-5,000 development)
    • Video conferencing (Zoom, already have likely)
    • Accounting integration (Stripe, PayPal for payments)

    Risk Management & Quality Control¶

    Quality Standards:¶

    1. Pre-travel checklist (2 weeks before)
    2. All bookings confirmed in writing
    3. Emergency contacts compiled
    4. Special requirements documented

    5. Client briefing completed

    6. During-travel monitoring

    7. Daily check-in (at least text/email)
    8. Proactive problem-solving

    9. Escalation protocol if issues arise

    10. Post-travel review

    11. Client satisfaction survey
    12. Vendor feedback
    13. Document lessons learned
    14. Update client profile for future trips

    Contingency Planning:¶

    • Travel insurance: Recommend/facilitate AIG, Allianz high-net-worth policies
    • Medical evacuation: Emergency contact & insurance coordination
    • Cancellation buffer: 2-week cancellation window for changes
    • Backup vendors: Identify alternative hotels/activities for every component

    Competitive Advantages of This Model¶

    ✅ Low capital requirements (outsource delivery) ✅ Scalable (add clients without hiring proportionally) ✅ Flexible (adjust services per client needs) ✅ Quality control (partner with best vendors globally) ✅ Margin-friendly (commission + markup model) ✅ Retention-focused (deep relationships, repeat business)

    Success Metrics to Track¶

    • Client acquisition cost (marketing spend per new client)
    • Average booking value (€X per trip)
    • Repeat rate (% of clients booking 2+ trips/year)
    • Referral rate (% of new clients from referrals)
    • Satisfaction score (NPS or post-trip survey)
    • Margin per booking (profit after commissions)
    • Time investment per client (hours/month to manage relationship)
