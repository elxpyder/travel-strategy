    Pricing Strategies: Premium UHNW Travel Service¶

    Executive Summary¶

    Three pricing models with distinct advantages/disadvantages. Recommended: Hybrid model (Strategy #2) for established agencies.

    STRATEGY 1: Commission-Only Model¶

    "Earn on volume—no upfront fees"

    Structure:¶

    • No service fees charged to clients
    • Revenue: Commission from hotel/activity/transport partners (10-20% average)
    • Example: €50K trip = €7,500 commission (15% average)
    • Client sees: Only the supplier costs, not your fee (transparent)

    Advantages:¶

    ✅ Easy sell — No resistance to premium fees
    ✅ Low client friction — Familiar to existing agency model
    ✅ Volume-friendly — Encourages multiple bookings
    ✅ Competitive — Positioned as "we make money only when you're happy"
    ✅ Scalable — More trips = more profit without renegotiating

    Disadvantages:¶

    ❌ Thin margins — 7.5-15% commission on €50K = €3,750-7,500 (not enough for premium service)
    ❌ Vendor dependent — Hotel/partner rates determine your income
    ❌ No value capture — Client unaware of premium curation work
    ❌ Incentive misalignment — Tempted to upsell quantity over quality
    ❌ Undervalues expertise — Curation work invisible to client

    Revenue Example (€50K trip):¶

    Component Cost Commission Your Revenue
    Hotels (€20K) €20K 12% = €2,400 €2,400
    Activities (€15K) €15K 20% = €3,000 €3,000
    Transport (€10K) €10K 8% = €800 €800
    Dining (€5K) €5K 0% (direct pay) €0
    Total €50K €6,200

    Your margin: 12.4% (€6,200 / €50K)
    Hours required: ~60 hours (discovery, curation, logistics, travel support)
    Hourly rate: €103/hour (below premium standard)

    STRATEGY 2: HYBRID MODEL (RECOMMENDED)¶

    "Membership + commission + markup"

    Structure:¶

    Option A: Tiered Membership + Commission - Entry tier: €2,500/year membership (access to planning service) - Includes: 2 trip consultations, 10% discount on curation fee - Target: Clients booking 1 trip/year

    • Premium tier: €7,500/year membership (concierge service)
    • Includes: Unlimited consultations, priority booking, 15% discount on curation fee

    • Target: Clients booking 2+ trips/year

    • Elite tier: €15,000/year membership (dedicated concierge + annual trip planning)

    • Includes: Dedicated account manager, priority everything, free annual trip research
    • Target: Clients booking 3+ trips/year, multi-generational family trips

    Plus: 8-12% markup on total trip cost (in addition to partner commissions)

    Revenue Example (€50K trip + annual membership):¶

    Scenario: New client, Premium tier (€7,500/year membership) | Component | Value | |-----------|-------| | Annual membership | €7,500 | | Trip package (€50K) × 12% markup | €6,000 | | Partner commissions | €6,200 | | Total annual revenue | €19,700 | | Hours: 80/year (planning + support) | | | Hourly rate | €246/hour ✅ |

    Advantages:¶

    ✅ Predictable revenue — Memberships = recurring annual income
    ✅ Premium positioning — Membership signals exclusive access
    ✅ Better margins — Membership (€2.5K-15K/year) + commissions + markup
    ✅ Client commitment — Membership = they're serious clients
    ✅ Retention focus — Membership renews annually, encourages loyalty
    ✅ Scales efficiently — 10 Premium-tier members = €75K/year baseline
    ✅ Justifiable fees — Membership clearly articulates value

    Disadvantages:¶

    ⚠️ Adoption friction — Some clients resist annual fees initially
    ⚠️ Complexity — Three tiers to explain and manage
    ⚠️ Commitment risk — Clients may not book after paying membership
    ⚠️ Tier cannibalization — Risk of underpricing Elite tier vs. Entry tier

    Revenue Scenarios (Hybrid Model, 10 clients):¶

    Conservative (4 Entry, 4 Premium, 2 Elite): - Memberships: (4 × €2.5K) + (4 × €7.5K) + (2 × €15K) = €80K/year - Average trip: €40K per client × 8 trips/year = €320K revenue - Markup (12%): €38.4K - Commissions (12% average): €38.4K - Total annual revenue: €156.8K - Profit (after costs): ~€85K

    Optimistic (2 Entry, 5 Premium, 3 Elite): - Memberships: (2 × €2.5K) + (5 × €7.5K) + (3 × €15K) = €107.5K/year - Average trip: €50K per client × 10 trips/year = €500K revenue - Markup (12%): €60K - Commissions (12% average): €60K - Total annual revenue: €227.5K - Profit (after costs): ~€130K

    STRATEGY 3: FEE-BASED MODEL¶

    "Premium service, premium pricing"

    Structure:¶

    • No membership (clients resist annual fees)
    • Service fee per booking: €3,000-7,500 (fixed or % of trip cost)
    • Plus: Partner commissions (normal rates)

    Pricing Tiers:¶

    Trip Value Service Fee As % of Trip
    €25K-40K €3,500 8-14%
    €40K-75K €5,000 6-12%
    €75K+ €7,500 8-10%

    Revenue Example (€50K trip):¶

    Component Value
    Service fee (€50K trip) €5,000
    Partner commissions €6,200
    Total revenue €11,200
    Your margin % 22.4%
    Hours: 60
    Hourly rate €187/hour

    Advantages:¶

    ✅ Simple — Single fee, easy to understand
    ✅ Transparent — Client pays for service explicitly
    ✅ Flexible — Works for one-off trips or repeat clients
    ✅ No commitment — Clients pay per trip, no membership lock-in
    ✅ Fair pricing — Fee scales with complexity (trip value)
    ✅ Industry familiar — Similar to traditional travel advisor fees

    Disadvantages:¶

    ❌ Price resistance — €5K service fee on €50K trip feels expensive upfront
    ❌ Unpredictable — Revenue depends on number of bookings
    ❌ One-time relationship — Less retention incentive
    ❌ Lower repeat rate — Clients comparison shop after first trip
    ❌ Harder to upsell — Fee creates friction for second/third trips
    ❌ No baseline income — Bad year = no bookings = €0 revenue

    STRATEGY 4: VALUE-BASED PRICING (Advanced)¶

    "We save you time/money—we share the savings"

    Structure:¶

    • Research phase: €2,000-5,000 upfront (non-refundable)
    • Commission + markup: Standard rates (12%)
    • Performance bonus: If you negotiate discounts/save money, client saves €X, you get 30% of savings

    Example:¶

    • Client wants €75K trip (estimated budget)
    • Your research finds better options, negotiates €60K package
    • Savings: €15K
    • Your bonus: 30% × €15K = €4,500
    • Total revenue: €5,000 research + €7,200 commissions + €4,500 bonus = €16,700

    Advantages:¶

    ✅ Alignment — Your success = client success
    ✅ High margins — Rewards expertise and negotiation skill
    ✅ Client satisfaction — They feel they got a deal
    ✅ Justifiable — Performance-based, not arbitrary

    Disadvantages:¶

    ❌ Complex — Difficult to calculate and explain
    ❌ Subjective — What counts as "savings"?
    ❌ Conflict potential — Client may dispute savings calculation
    ❌ Not suitable for everyone — Luxury clients want experiences, not deals
    ❌ Risky — Revenue depends on your negotiation skills

    RECOMMENDATION: HYBRID MODEL (Strategy #2)¶

    Why This Works Best for You:¶

    1. Established agency strength — You have infrastructure + existing clients
    2. Leverage this with membership tiers

    3. Existing clients are natural upsell candidates

    4. UHNW client psychology

    5. They expect "membership access" (clubs, private banks, luxury cards)
    6. Membership = exclusivity and commitment

    7. Removes price objections (they're paying for access, not per transaction)

    8. Revenue stability

    9. Memberships provide baseline income
    10. Commissions + markup layer on top

    11. Predictable enough to hire staff, invest in marketing

    12. Retention focus

    13. Annual renewal drives retention
    14. Encourages repeated bookings (maximize value of membership)

    15. Easier to expand to family members, friends (referrals)

    16. Pricing power

    17. Three tiers allows value segmentation
    18. Entry tier captures price-sensitive clients
    19. Elite tier captures truly committed families
    20. Upgrade path clear (start Entry, move to Premium)

    IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP¶

    Month 1: Soft Launch¶

    • Target: Existing agency clients + warm referrals
    • Offer: Premium tier only (€7,500/year) for first 3-5 clients
    • Messaging: "Exclusive new premium concierge service—limited availability"
    • Goal: Proof of concept, gather testimonials

    Month 2-3: Formal Launch¶

    • Introduce: All three tiers (Entry, Premium, Elite)
    • Marketing: Client acquisition campaigns
    • Pricing adjustments: Based on early feedback

    Month 6 Review:¶

    • Target: 5-8 paying members
    • Evaluate: Tier mix (is Elite working? Upgrade rate?)
    • Adjust: Prices, benefits, positioning as needed

    Final Pricing Recommendation¶

    Tiered Membership (Hybrid Model):¶

    ENTRY TIER: "Travel Advisor" - Annual fee: €2,500 - Includes: 2 custom trip consultations, standard booking service - Target: Clients booking 1 trip/year, value-conscious - Commissions + markup: Standard rates

    PREMIUM TIER: "Travel Concierge" ← START HERE - Annual fee: €7,500 - Includes: Unlimited consultations, priority booking, VIP treatment, dedicated account manager - Target: Clients booking 2-3 trips/year, main target segment - Commissions + markup: Standard rates + 15% discount on service fees

    ELITE TIER: "Travel Curator" - Annual fee: €15,000 - Includes: Dedicated concierge manager, complimentary annual expedition research, white-glove treatment, family extended services - Target: Clients booking 3+ trips/year, multi-generational families - Commissions + markup: Standard rates + complimentary research/planning

    Launch with Premium Tier (€7,500/year) for first 6 months, then introduce Entry and Elite based on demand.
